# 🏠 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard | Power BI 
Created By :Danish Alam

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes Airbnb listings, hosts, reviews, pricing, ratings, seasonality, and host verification across **10 major cities**. The objective is to identify key business trends, understand customer behavior, compare city-level performance, and generate actionable insights from Airbnb data.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

- Analyze Airbnb listing growth over time
- Compare market share across major cities
- Analyze average prices by property type
- Evaluate customer ratings across different cities
- Understand customer review frequency
- Identify seasonal travel patterns
- Analyze host identity verification and trust signals
- Identify major cities driving Airbnb activity

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 1. Listing Growth & Business Lifecycle

The first section analyzes the growth of Airbnb listings over time and divides the business journey into different stages:

- Introduction
- Growth
- Maturity
- Decline
- Reinvention
- COVID-19

### Key Insights

- Airbnb reached its **highest number of new listings in 2015**.
- Listing growth experienced restraint during **2016 and 2017**, partly due to tightening local regulations.
- Airbnb became profitable in the **second half of 2016**.
- 2017 marked the first full year of generating income.
- Growth resumed from 2018 before being disrupted by the COVID-19 period.

---

# 🌍 2. Market Share by City

The dashboard compares Airbnb activity across:

- Paris
- New York
- Sydney
- Rome
- Rio de Janeiro
- Istanbul
- Mexico City
- Bangkok
- Cape Town
- Hong Kong

### Key Insights

**Paris, New York, and Sydney account for almost half of the total listings and 59% of total reviews.**

Paris has the highest number of listings and reviews among the analyzed cities.

The dashboard also compares the share of **Superhost vs. Non-Superhost** listings and presents the cumulative market contribution of each city.

# 💰 3. Average Price by Property Type

The dashboard compares average prices across different Airbnb property types.

| Property Type | Average Price |

---

# ⭐ 4. Ratings Analysis

The dashboard evaluates cities using multiple rating dimensions:

- Accuracy
- Cleanliness
- Communication
- Location
- Value for Money

### Key Insights

- **Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro** are among the best-rated cities overall.
- **Hong Kong and Istanbul** have comparatively lower overall ratings.
- **Cleanliness and Value for Money** are generally the lower-scoring rating dimensions.
- Mexico City records particularly strong ratings across the measured categories.

---

# 🔄 5. Review Frequency

The dashboard analyzes how frequently customers leave reviews.

### Key Insights

- **86.5%** of reviews are associated with customers who reviewed only once.
- **98.8%** of customers reviewed **3 times or less**.
- One customer is shown with **283 reviews**, with the last two reviews dated **13-Oct-2020** for two listings in Bangkok.

This analysis helps understand customer engagement and repeat-review behavior.

---

# 🌦️ 6. Seasonality Analysis

The dashboard examines the percentage of monthly reviews across:

- Mexico City
- New York
- Paris
- Rome
- Sydney

### Key Insights

- **Paris and Rome dominate review share from April to August**, reflecting the European summer travel period.
- **New York experiences increased review activity during November and December**, corresponding with the holiday season.

---

# 🛡️ 7. Host Trust & Verification

The dashboard analyzes host identity verification and profile-picture availability as trust signals.

### Key Insights

- **66.9%** of hosts have both a profile picture and verified identity.
- **32.6%** have a profile picture but are not identity verified.
- **0.1%** are identity verified without a profile picture.
- **0.3%** have neither a profile picture nor verified identity.

Overall, the dashboard indicates that the majority of hosts provide at least one trust signal.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

Based on the analysis, several important business insights emerge:

### 1. Market Concentration
Paris, New York, and Sydney represent a significant portion of Airbnb's listing and review activity.

### 2. Pricing Opportunity
Hotel rooms command the highest average price, while private rooms provide a more affordable accommodation option.

### 3. Customer Experience
Cleanliness and value for money appear to be important areas where cities can improve their customer experience.

### 4. Seasonal Demand
European cities show strong activity during the summer months, while New York experiences stronger activity during the holiday season.

### 5. Customer Engagement
The majority of customers leave reviews only once, indicating an opportunity to improve repeat customer engagement.

### 6. Host Trust
A large majority of hosts have at least one visible trust signal, such as a profile picture or verified identity.
